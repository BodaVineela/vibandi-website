About

About Vibandi

Vibandi LLC was founded to be a single home for several businesses. Instead of starting a new company for every idea, each venture becomes a division of Vibandi, with its own focus and the shared backing of one organization.

Technology is the first division. Others, including media and social channels, will follow as they launch.

  • Built to lastEvery venture is started with the long term in mind.
  • One standard across every divisionWhatever the business, the same care goes into it.
  • Room to growThe company is set up so new ventures can join without starting over.
